# Design

I didn't want Campus Customs to feel like a plain template or a serious corporate store. I wanted it to feel like a cozy little shop on Broadway with a personality, so people enjoy browsing and stay long enough to buy something.

**Colors:** I went with warm earthy tones: oat and cream backgrounds, terracotta for prices and buttons, mustard for tags, sage green for "in stock," and a soft Yale blue for the top bar and footer. My first version was too bright, so I made it lighter and easier on the eyes. Warm colors feel welcoming and keep the photos as the main thing on the page, which is what we're selling.

**Fonts:** Big rounded serif headings (Fraunces) give it a friendly boutique feel. The body text is a clean sans serif (DM Sans), so prices and descriptions are easy to read. A handwritten font (Caveat) shows up for little side notes like "bestie" and "Buddy approved." It feels like a real person runs the shop, which builds trust.

**Personality:** Buddy the bulldog is the mascot and the chatbot ("Head of Vibes"). The copy is playful: "Back to the closet," "Warning: may cause spontaneous school spirit," and a scrolling banner with lines like "Finals week = hoodie week." People remember a shop that makes them smile, and they're more likely to come back.

**Product photos:** Some of the original photos were blurry or had dark shadows, so we cleaned every one into a clear cut-out on the same soft background. Every card now looks consistent and the clothes are easy to see. Bad photos make people trust a shop less, so this mattered to me.

**Product cards:** Every card is the same size, with a soft outline and a small offset shadow, so the grid looks neat. The size strip and the "Low stock" badges sit right on the card, so shoppers see what's available before they click, and the badges add a little honest urgency.

**Product page:** A big photo on the left, and on the right the price in large terracotta, the colors, and big size buttons that show how many are left. Sold-out sizes are crossed out. A "You might also like" row at the bottom keeps people browsing instead of leaving.

**Chat box:** The chat is a friendly bubble with Buddy's face. The button does a little wiggle every few seconds so people notice it, and there are one-tap suggested questions so nobody has to guess what to ask. When Buddy finds products, the cards "deal in" onto the page with a small animation, which makes the search feel fun and connects the chat to the shop.

**Small motion, used carefully:** The bulldog on the Home page bobs a little, and cards pop in gently. All animation turns off for people whose computer is set to reduce motion, so it stays comfortable for everyone.
